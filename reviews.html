Tips From Frequent Customers

Practical recommendations based on real experiences shared by our frequent visitors.

🏬

Explore Both Sections

We recommend taking time to browse both the textile wing and the separate supermarket floor. It makes it convenient to purchase groceries and festive clothes under one roof.

🎄

Check Collections Early

Diwali, Pongal, Christmas, and Valentine's displays sell out quickly. Visit a couple of weeks before the holiday to get access to the widest color ranges and early seasonal discounts.

🙋‍♂️

Ask Staff For Help

Staff members like Karthik are experts on inventory location. If you are in a rush, describe what you need, and they will locate it instantly across the multiple showroom floors.

💰

Look For Bundled Offers

Regular shoppers recommend asking about bundle packs, like 2 tops for ₹550 and 2 leggings for ₹300. There are always budget-friendly bundles active on daily clothing.

🧵

Buy Uniforms In Bulk

For weddings and family functions, buying uniform sarees together ensures identical batches, matching print patterns, and significant bulk discount rates.

🎁

Stock Up During Schemes

Ask the sales counters about our monthly gold purchase scheme drives and seasonal lottery gifts. These offer great long-term value for local family budgets.
